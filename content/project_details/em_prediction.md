<p class="abstract-text" lang="en">
In this student project, I investigated how well different data sources and AI models could forecast the UEFA EURO 2024. The goal was to compare simulations based on FIFA23 team statistics with predictions generated by large language models, including ChatGPT, Gemini, and Llama3.
</p>

<img class="project-medium-image" src="/images/projects/fifa.jpg" alt="FIFA23">

<p class="abstract-text" lang="en">
For this, I combined FIFA23 team data with additional information from Transfermarkt to create performance scores for each participating nation. These scores were then used to simulate the full tournament, including the group stage, knockout rounds, extra time, and penalty shootouts. To make the results more robust, the tournament was simulated 10,000 times.
</p>

<img class="project-large-image" src="/images/projects/fifa_pred.png" alt="FIFA23 tournament prediction results">

<p class="abstract-text" lang="en">
In addition, I generated comparable performance scores using different LLMs and evaluated whether newer or more advanced language models produced better forecasts. The predicted tournament outcomes were compared with the actual EURO 2024 results using classification metrics, permutation tests, and Spearman correlation.
</p>

<p class="abstract-text" lang="en">
Overall, the FIFA23-based simulation performed best, correctly predicting the champion, while the LLM-based forecasts showed limited predictive power. The project highlighted that domain-specific structured data can be more useful for sports forecasting than general-purpose language models alone.
</p>
